### **1. Seismic Anisotropy TTI Model for the Alaska Continent**
A TTI model including dip angle estimates (two-layer model) for the anisotropic foliation plane across the Alaska continent.  

📥 Download Model: [Zenodo DOI](https://zenodo.org/records/10723710)

---

### **2. Seismic Azimuthal Anisotropy Model for the Juan de Fuca‐Gorda Plate System**
A two-layered seismic azimuthal anisotropy model for the Juan de Fuca‐Gorda Plate System, derived from Rayleigh wave dispersion measurements (10–85 s).  

📥 Download Model: [Zenodo DOI](https://zenodo.org/records/14031390)

---

### **3. Surface Wave Isotropic and Azimuthally Anisotropic Dispersion Across Alaska and the Aleutian Subduction Zone**
A surface wave dispersion dataset that includes **isotropic and anisotropic** phase velocity measurements for both Rayleigh and Love waves.  

📥 Download Model: [Zenodo DOI](https://zenodo.org/records/10976561)

---

### **4. Seismic Azimuthal Anisotropy Model Beneath the Aleutian Subduction Zone**
A **three-layered** seismic azimuthal anisotropy model for the **forearc region** of the Aleutian subduction zone, based on Rayleigh wave dispersion (10–85 s).

📥 Download Model: [Zenodo DOI](https://zenodo.org/records/10975885)

---

### **5. Layered Seismic Anisotropy Model of the Anatolian Plate**

**a. Three-layered seismic azimuthal anisotropy model**
A three-layered seismic azimuthal anisotropy model for the Anatolian Plate, based on Rayleigh wave dispersion (8–80 s).

**b. Receiver function A1max anisotropy**
Receiver function harmonic decomposition results showing crustal anisotropy (A1max) across the Anatolian Plate.

**c. Shear wave splitting models**
Shear wave splitting one-layer and two-layer models for the Anatolian Plate region.

📥 Download Model: [Zenodo DOI](https://zenodo.org/records/17306944)
